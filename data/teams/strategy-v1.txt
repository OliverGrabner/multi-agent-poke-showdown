# Strategy teams for multi battles: four doubles archetypes, level 50, no legendaries.
# Each team is one 6-Pokémon doubles team split between two partners (3 each, lead first).
# The splits put each archetype's combo across the two partners, so it only works if they coordinate.
# Built from standard Gen 9 doubles cores (Smogon Doubles OU, VGC): see docs/DECISIONS.md.
