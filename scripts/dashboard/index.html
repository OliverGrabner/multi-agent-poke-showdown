Does talking make LLM teammates win more?

Pokémon Showdown Multi Battles, Gen 9 random teams. Qwen3.8-27B (reasoning effort medium) on side p1+p3. Every condition plays the same 25 team draws twice with sides swapped. Built from finished battles.

1. Win rate against the max-power bot

Dot = win rate, line = 95% Wilson interval, dashed = 50%. Crashed battles (server timeouts) are excluded.

2. Same teams, battle by battle (free talk vs each other condition)

Battles played by both conditions on the same seed and side. only free talk won both won both lost only the other won. p = exact two-sided McNemar test on the discordant battles.

3. Head to head

Both teams Qwen. A "draw pair" is one team draw played twice with sides swapped; only pairs where both battles finished are counted.

4. Win rate vs tokens generated

Output tokens per battle (thinking + messages), Qwen side only. Talking teams also think more, so part of the gain may come from extra thinking.

5. How the teams play

Focus fire = both attacked the same foe, out of turns where both attacked a foe. Protect / speed control = used, out of choices where such a move was available (Protect, Detect, Spiky Shield, ...; Tailwind, Trick Room, Icy Wind, Thunder Wave, ...). Switches = out of all choices. KOs/turn = foes fainted per turn when both attacked, split by focus vs split.
